# Pilot Selection Plan

The pilot contains exactly 20 fully annotated records. Its job is to expose schema gaps, not to prove a complete Avedon style model.

## Subset balance

- 10 records from `formal_portrait_general`
- 10 records from `in_the_american_west`

If an otherwise valid structural class is absent from one subset, do not fabricate balance. Record the absence and select an additional eligible work from the other subset.

## Structural coverage targets

Across the 20 records, aim for:

- at least 3 face-detail or tight-head examples;
- at least 5 head-and-shoulders or bust examples;
- at least 4 waist-up examples;
- at least 5 three-quarter or full-body examples;
- up to 3 uncommon-view examples such as profile, over-shoulder, back view, or face fragment, but only when authoritative eligible works actually exist.

Categories may overlap. Do not include an out-of-scope image merely to fill a target.

## Additional variation

The selected set should collectively include:

- low, medium, and high visible expression intensity;
- no hands, one hand, both hands, and partial hands where available;
- frontal, three-quarter, and profile head views where available;
- centered and offset subject placement;
- several decades of eligible mature portrait work in the general subset;
- visible variation in crop edge, negative space, light, and tonal contrast.

## Exit test

After annotation:

1. Re-read every field definition without looking at the existing values.
2. Re-annotate at least four structurally different records.
3. Compare the two passes and identify ambiguous fields.
4. Revise definitions before adding more records.
5. Promote no route unless its evidence passes `supported-routes.md` criteria.
