# Shared Style DNA

These claims are limited to the three supported V1 routes. They are not a summary of Avedon's complete career.

## Evidence rule

For every claimed invariant, record:

```text
claim:
applies_to:
supporting_records:
contradicting_records:
confidence:
operational_instruction:
```

Do not promote museum prose alone into a complete generation rule. Use institutional descriptions to establish context, then verify the visual behavior through annotated corpus records.

## 1. Context is removed, not replaced with a fictional biography

- claim: Supported routes isolate one sitter against a continuous controlled background without readable location information.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes.
- supporting_records: `AP-EXP-MUS-01`, `AP-EXP-MUS-07`, `AP-PIL-CAR-03`, `AP-EXP-CAR-14`, `AP-EXP-CAR-01`, `AP-EXP-CAR-20`.
- contradicting_records: none inside the supported exact keys.
- confidence: high.
- operational_instruction: remove environmental detail and backdrop edges; do not replace them with invented occupation, era, props, dirt, costume, or status markers.

## 2. Black-and-white is stable in the supported routes

- claim: Every route-eligible record in the three supported exact keys is black-and-white.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes.
- supporting_records: `AP-PIL-MUS-03`, `AP-EXP-FND-10`, `AP-PIL-CAR-06`, `AP-EXP-CAR-32`, `AP-EXP-CAR-11`, `AP-EXP-CAR-27`.
- contradicting_records: none inside the supported exact keys.
- confidence: high.
- operational_instruction: render neutral black-and-white with retained skin, hair, and clothing separation; do not apply sepia, color accents, or crushed monochrome that erases identity detail.

## 3. The camera relationship is frontal, centered, and direct

- claim: All 34 route-eligible records in the three supported exact keys place the sitter centrally; every head is frontal, and 33 of 34 gaze toward the camera.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes.
- supporting_records: `AP-EXP-FND-08`, `AP-EXP-MUS-08`, `AP-PIL-CAR-07`, `AP-EXP-CAR-32`, `AP-EXP-CAR-02`, `AP-EXP-CAR-23`.
- contradicting_records: `AP-EXP-CAR-28` has eyes closed during an open expression.
- confidence: high for placement and head view; high with one valid variation for gaze.
- operational_instruction: preserve the input's already-frontal head and center the subject without changing its crop. Preserve closed eyes if present; do not force eye contact by editing eyelids or gaze.

## 4. Expression is observed, not standardized into severity

- claim: Neutral closed mouths dominate, but smiles, parted lips, and open expression occur within supported routes.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes.
- supporting_records: neutral `AP-EXP-MUS-04`, `AP-EXP-CAR-14`, `AP-EXP-CAR-13`; smile `AP-PIL-MUS-04`, `AP-EXP-MUS-03`; parted lips `AP-PIL-CAR-03`, `AP-PIL-CAR-10`; open expression `AP-EXP-CAR-28`.
- contradicting_records: none; the variation is the finding.
- confidence: high.
- operational_instruction: preserve the input's mouth state and expression intensity. Do not manufacture a blank, stern, smiling, or theatrical expression to signal “Avedon.”

## 5. Hands and sitter-authored gesture remain variable

- claim: Supported records range from no visible hands to both hands, resting, touching the body, holding clothing or an existing object, and active gesture.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes.
- supporting_records: no hands `AP-EXP-MUS-04`, `AP-PIL-CAR-06`; resting `AP-EXP-FND-12`, `AP-EXP-CAR-01`; touching body `AP-EXP-FND-17`, `AP-EXP-CAR-17`; existing object `AP-PIL-MUS-03`, `AP-EXP-CAR-20`; gesture `AP-EXP-CAR-28`.
- contradicting_records: none; the variation is the finding.
- confidence: high.
- operational_instruction: preserve the input's visible-hand count, hand-to-body relation, and pose-defining object. Never add a corpus object's narrative meaning to the user.

## 6. Light is broad and descriptive; contrast remains printable

- claim: The general supported route has frontal soft light in all 15 records; the visually resolved IAW pilot records use frontal soft-to-medium light. Supported routes retain medium-to-high overall contrast rather than low-contrast haze.
- applies_to: all supported V1 routes, with lower confidence for exact IAW light direction because many expanded Carter images were insufficient for that field.
- supporting_records: general `AP-PIL-MUS-03`, `AP-EXP-FND-08`, `AP-EXP-MUS-07`; IAW `AP-PIL-CAR-01`, `AP-PIL-CAR-03`, `AP-PIL-CAR-06`, `AP-PIL-CAR-09`.
- contradicting_records: none among resolved supported-route observations; unresolved fields are not counted as confirmation.
- confidence: high for general, medium for IAW.
- operational_instruction: use broad frontal light, soft-to-medium facial shadows, and medium-to-high tonal contrast; retain pores, wrinkles, garment texture, and deep blacks without clipping the face.

## Print-edge note

Black print borders occur throughout the supported evidence (`AP-PIL-MUS-03`, `AP-EXP-MUS-10`, `AP-PIL-CAR-03`, `AP-EXP-CAR-01`). They describe the photographed print or scan presentation, not the sitter's scene. Do not add a black frame by default; add it only when the user asks for an Avedon-print presentation.
